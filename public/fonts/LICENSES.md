# Web fonts

All fonts are self-hosted, subset to the characters this site uses and sliced by `unicode-range` (`scripts/build-fonts.py`). Every font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), which allows subsetting and redistribution. The `BG *` names are CSS family aliases for the subset files; the original font metadata is kept.

| Family | Role | Source font | Copyright |
|---|---|---|---|
| BG Display | Book titles, headings, region names | Noto Serif CJK SC SemiBold | © 2017 Adobe (Source Han Serif) / Google |
| BG Serif | Long-form lead text | Noto Serif CJK SC Regular | © 2017 Adobe / Google |
| BG Kai | Hooks, librarian, reading hypotheses | LXGW WenKai 1.520 | © 2021 LXGW, based on Klee One © Fontworks |
| BG Latin | Foreign titles, Latin display | Cormorant Garamond Italic | © 2015 Christian Thalmann |
| BG Mono | Indices, counts, kicker lines | IBM Plex Mono | © 2017 IBM Corp. |
